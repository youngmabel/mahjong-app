# American Mahjong Apps and Websites: What Exists, What Works, What Is Missing

> **Note added after the independent fact check (research/factcheck.md):** where this file and the main notes (01 to 08) differ, the main notes carry the corrected version.

Research date: October 26, 2026. Scope: existing apps and websites for American (NMJL) mahjong, so our solo learn-and-practice app can borrow what works and fill the gaps.

## How to read this file

Confidence tags used below:

- **High**: official store listing, developer help page, NMJL website, or the official NMJL Online FAQ, read directly.
- **Medium**: a reputable secondary source (Tom Sloper, a law firm), or a developer's own claim that could not be cross-checked.
- **Low**: a competitor's comparison blog, an aggregator site, a single review or forum post, or sources that conflict.

Method and limits. Sources were App Store and Google Play listings, developer help pages, the NMJL website, the official NMJL Online FAQ forum, Tom Sloper's sloperama.com, two law-firm articles, competitor comparison blogs (flagged as such), and the reviews visible on store pages. Reddit blocked automated access. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so later leads could only be followed through pages already found. No app was installed or played. Feature descriptions come from listings and documentation, not hands-on testing. Store pages show about ten reviews each, so review themes are indicative, not representative.

## 1. Bottom line

1. **The field is crowded and young.** Long-running options are Real Mah Jongg (iOS since July 2017), Eight Bam's American Mahjong Practice (March 2019), I Love Mahj's online game (December 2020), Mahjong 4 Friends (2021), and MyJongg.net (about 2013). On top of those, at least a dozen new American mahjong apps launched between January and October 2026, many aimed at beginners. Details and sources for this summary are in sections 2 to 6.
2. **The official game does not show the card.** NMJL Online is built by Quicksilver Software, with design and project management by the League (Tom Sloper is on that team). It requires a paid subscription plus the physical card, and its FAQ says there is no in-game card. Its iOS app had 1.8 stars from four reviews in October 2026.
3. **"Suggested hands" is now standard.** Nearly every game app lists your closest card lines with a tiles-away count. These panels rank by tile overlap, often ignore dead tiles, and tell you what to aim for but not why.
4. **Beginners stumble on a predictable set of things:** the card's color-coding, joker limits, concealed hands, when a call is legal, finding the joker swap, and not knowing why the game blocked a move.
5. **The main gap.** No app found combines scripted tutorial scenarios, guided matches that explain strategy, and a planned fading of help ending in free play. The closest pieces are I Love Mahj's Practice Mode, The Mahj Index's Guided and Classic modes, NMJL Online's Safety ON/OFF, and lesson-first apps with bots added on.
6. **Licensing.** No public report of NMJL enforcement against an app or website was found. Apps fall into five patterns: the official app, which hides the card; "bring your own card" apps; apps where users enter or photograph their own card; apps with an original card only; and apps with built-in current-year hands and no stated license (section 6).

### 2.3 Notes on the most important options

**NMJL Online (official).** The League has run an official online game for years. In 2014 it was by Sigma Software, Windows plus browser, $45 per year, and required a membership number from the card ([Linda Fisher's blog, Feb 9, 2014](http://mahjblog.blogspot.com/2014/02/official-nmjl-mahjongg-on-ipads-and.html); Medium). The current version credits "Software Design and Development by Quicksilver Software" and "Design and Project Management by The National Mah Jongg League" (Tom Sloper, Larry Unger, David Unger) ([credits, April 24, 2024](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=62); High). A February 2025 features video mentions a free trial through April 30, 2025, with an iPad and iPhone version in development ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VrChTJPkX0I)). The iOS app FAQ dates from July 2026 ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=138)).

Design points worth noting, all High (official FAQ written by "tsloper_admin"):
- **Rules.** NMJL rules only. No Hot Wall, Cold Wall, Blanks, Messy, Pischke, or Picking Ahead; contested discards are awarded automatically; seats rotate per *Mah Jongg Made Easy* ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=90)).
- **Safety ON/OFF**, chosen by the host. ON lights the Mahj and Call buttons only when the move is legal, and enables Auto Pass. OFF keeps Mahj lit so you can make mistakes and be penalized, on the stated theory that "We learn by making mistakes" ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=89)).
- **Bots** are personalities, not a numeric scale. Names cue the type: Beginner bots are Betty, Bianca, Barbara; Advanced bots are Alice, Angie, Andy ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=98)).
- **Charleston.** Tap tiles to raise them; the Pass button activates once the required count is raised ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=124)).
- **Tile reading.** A magnifier enlarges any discard or exposed tile on hover or tap, and helps target a joker when dragging a tile onto it ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=101)).
- **Accessibility.** A Big Number tileset, four high-contrast schemes, and text-to-speech chat ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=82)).
- **Tutorials.** Four short ones: Welcome (tiles, card, a turn); How to Play (calls, exposures, joker redemption); The Charleston and the Card; Death Challenges ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=88)).

The four iOS reviews (dated September and October; the app reached iOS in 2026) are harsh: landscape-only, "impossible to exit," a keyboard that will not close, login failures, a dated look, and a buyer who expected online access to the card and did not get it ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nmjl-online/id6476651173?see-all=reviews); High that these reviews exist, Low as a quality measure with n=4).

**Real Mah Jongg.** The most established subscription app, live since July 2017 ([App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/real-mah-jongg/id1249840399); High). It is strong for social play: live tables, bots filling seats, and voice and text chat. Its learning aid is Hand Suggestions. A forum user called it "good for practicing online" but said it does not teach the game ([Quarter to Three forum, May 29, 2025](https://forum.quartertothree.com/t/american-mah-jong/155959); Low).

Reviews describe the calling interface as auto-call prompts plus a "may call" indicator. One reviewer asked for a short call window instead of constant pauses, and new players found calls "inconsistent" until the developer explained the matching-tile requirement ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/real-mah-jongg/id1249840399?see-all=reviews); Medium).

**Mahjong 4 Friends.** The free, high-volume option, with about 26K iOS ratings at 4.9. Its documentation is unusually detailed ([docs](https://mahjong4friends.com/pages/faqs/full_documentation.html); High):
- **Calls and Charleston.** You tap tiles into a "placemat," then press Proceed. Every player must press Proceed on each discard, with a counter such as "Proceed 3/4."
- **Rack.** Auto-Sort by suit and number is on by default; it can be turned off for manual sorting.
- **Suggested Hands** show how many tiles away each hand is. The docs warn the list "ignores discards," so some listed hands may be impossible.
- **Opponent analysis.** Clicking an opponent's exposures shows their possible hands.
- **Undo.** "Revert" rolls play back; "History" restarts from any turn.
- **Hints off.** One checkbox disables all hints.

The October 2026 update added portrait mode ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1552704332)).

**I Love Mahj.** The most complete learning ecosystem around a game, but browser-only and paid after a two-week trial. It separates "Just-for-Fun" games, where Practice Mode can be switched on with Level 1 bots, from "Official" games, and tells learners to move up when ready ([learn page](https://ilovemahj.com/learn-american-mah-jongg); High). The Exercise Room drills making each hand from tiles, naming a hand from a few tiles, and deducing hands from exposures ([learn page](https://ilovemahj.com/learn-american-mah-jongg)), plus a Charleston Practice exercise ([card choice KB](https://ilovemahj.com/kb/67000660954)); High.

It handles the "bots cheat" complaint with numbers: with three bots and about 10% wall games, an equally skilled human should win about 22.5% of games, which matches the platform average ([KB](https://ilovemahj.com/kb/67000755046); High).

**American Mahjong Practice (Eight Bam).** The strongest pure solo drilling tool. By default its bots never declare Mah Jongg, so a learner can always finish a hand; turning on "bots that win" is framed as defense practice. Other strengths: section filters, a "play the whole card" win filter, Replay and Undo, and an "Open Play" switch that turns off NMJL rules ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1458051621); High). Its old FAQ told beginners to learn basic gameplay elsewhere first ([AppFollow copy of the description](https://appfollow.io/ios/american-mahjong-practice/1458051621?country=us); Medium). Versions from 9.30 on show the bots' hands after each game ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/american-mahjong-practice/id1458051621?see-all=reviews); Medium). On Android, Eight Bam has published a separate app per card year, such as "American Mahjong Practice 2022" ([Apptopia](https://apptopia.com/google-play/app/com.eightbam.americanmahjongpractice2022/about); Medium).

**Mahj Parlour.** The most polished lessons-plus-bots app of 2026, with about 1.2K ratings at 4.9 within six months. It shows the risk of the "bring your own card" approach. A reviewer who wants to love it rarely plays because the physical card is needed, and asks for an in-app way to look up hands ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761298326?see-all=reviews); High that the review exists). Another reviewer likes the pinned suggestion precisely because it lets them play while traveling without the card.

## 3. Interface conventions the field has settled on

**Card display.** There are four approaches:
- **No card in the game:** NMJL Online, Mahj Parlour (suggestions only), and MyJongg.net for any non-MyJongg card.
- **Built-in hands on screen:** Eight Bam (a reviewer praises seeing the yearly card on screen), Callie's (searchable card), and Bam Good Time (shows the printed card line).
- **User-supplied card:** you photograph your own card and the app reads it (Jade Table, Callie's).
- **Original card:** I Love Mahj card, MyJongg Starter Card, Steam's original card plus Card Builder, American Mahjongg Classic's editor.

**Suggested hands and tiles-away.** Standard practice is a ranked list of the closest lines with a count of tiles still needed.
- **Pinning** a target above the rack: Mahj Parlour, Maylee.
- **Concealed marker and point value:** Bam Good Time, Mahj Parlour.
- **Section filters:** Eight Bam.
- **Win tracking by section:** Mahjong 4 Friends, Eight Bam, Mahj Parlour.
- **Known weakness:** most lists ignore dead tiles (Mahjong 4 Friends docs, MyJongg's Tile Tutor docs, an Eight Bam review, and a Maylee review about "% to win"). Mahjong Mate and Jade Table instead compute odds of completion.

**Rule enforcement modes.**
- **Strict mode with a "learn by mistakes" switch:** NMJL Online's Safety ON/OFF.
- **House-rules mode:** Eight Bam's Open Play.
- **Hints switch:** Mahjong 4 Friends' Disable Hints.
- **Explained blocks:** Bam Good Time (banners added August to September 2026) and Mahjong 4 Friends show why a call is blocked. Reviewers single out this feature as a learning aid ([M4F reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-4-friends-multiplayer/id1552704332?see-all=reviews)).

**Calls and exposures.**
- **Legal-only Call button:** NMJL Online with Safety ON.
- **Placemat plus Proceed:** Mahjong 4 Friends.
- **Claim, select tiles, then Expose:** MyJongg, where an exposure must be made all at once and cannot be edited later.
- **Explanatory banner and cues:** Bam Good Time says what a tap will do and warns before you spend jokers.
- **Waiting indicator:** NMJL Online shows a three-dot indicator while another player is deciding on a discard.
- **Pacing complaints:** reviewers object to frequent pauses (Real Mah Jongg) and call windows that are too short or cover the hand (The Mahj App).

**Joker and blank swaps.**
- **Discoverability is the weak spot:** two Maylee reviewers could not find the joker swap (a top-right button that lights orange), and a Mahj Parlour reviewer could not find the blank swap.
- **Good aids:** Bam Good Time's cue names the rack and color; MyJongg fades and red-outlines jokers that can no longer be redeemed; NMJL Online's magnifier helps when dragging a tile onto a joker.

**Charleston.**
- **Tap to raise, then Pass:** NMJL Online.
- **Placemat with instruction bubbles each round:** Mahjong 4 Friends.
- **Blind passes:** the game fills the remainder (MyJongg); zero-tile blind passes are allowed (Bam Good Time).
- **Suggestions during the Charleston:** shown on screen in Mahj Parlour.
- **Dedicated Charleston trainers** exist outside the main games: I Love Mahj's exercise, The Mahj Index drill, Order of the Tile games, mahjongg.ai.studio's assistant and daily puzzle, and Jade Table's timed tracker.

**Rack sorting.**
- **Auto-sort by default with a manual option:** Mahjong 4 Friends.
- **Auto-sort at game start, flowers first:** Maylee.
- **Free tile movement:** Eight Bam.
- **Readable discard area:** sorted discards are an option (Mahjong 4 Friends); a reviewer praised grouped discards (The Mahjong App).

**Undo, replay, review.**
- **Undo:** Replay and Undo (Eight Bam); Revert and History (Mahjong 4 Friends).
- **Bot hands revealed:** after the game (Eight Bam); after practice games (Mahj Parlour).
- **Deeper review:** post-game analysis and a replay viewer (Jade Table).
- **Official app:** NMJL Online's FAQ mentions no undo or review.

**Bots.**
- **Personality types:** NMJL Online (seven), Callie's (32), American Mahjongg Classic (20 across 5 levels).
- **Numeric levels:** 3 at I Love Mahj; 5 at Bam Good Time, each favoring different hands; Easy and Normal at Mahj Parlour; a slider up to "Superhuman" at Mahjong 4 Friends.
- **Non-winning bots by default:** Eight Bam.

**Accessibility.** Big-number tiles, high contrast, and a magnifier (NMJL Online); a large-tile font (Eight Bam); a High Visibility tile set (Mahj Parlour); large text and tile options added in The Mahj App 5.2. Small tiles and buttons on phones remain a top complaint (section 4).

## 4. What users praise and complain about

### 4.1 Praise (what helps learners)

- **Suggested hands with tile counts, and seeing the card on screen.** This is the most frequent praise. Examples: an Eight Bam reviewer says they learned the basics in a day and credits the on-screen yearly card; another uses section filters to focus practice ([Eight Bam reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/american-mahjong-practice/id1458051621?see-all=reviews)). A Mahjong 4 Friends reviewer says suggestions during the Charleston plus viewing opponents' exposures helped them learn the card quickly ([M4F reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-4-friends-multiplayer/id1552704332?see-all=reviews)). Confidence Medium (consistent across apps).
- **Being told why a move is blocked.** Mahjong 4 Friends reviewers say bots flag disallowed moves and the app explains blocks, which taught them the rules (same source). Medium.
- **Patient, always-available practice.** Several reviewers use bots between weekly games (Real Mah Jongg, Mahjong 4 Friends, The Mahjong App). I Love Mahj markets bots that "don't mind" slow play ([St. Louis Jewish Light, Aug 2021](https://stljewishlight.org/news/news-local/this-week-in-mah-jongg-lets-play)). Medium.
- **Offline play and responsive developers** (Eight Bam, Mahjong 4 Friends reviews). Medium.

### 4.2 Complaints

- **Unrealistic or unfair-seeming bots.** Real Mah Jongg reviewers say bots complete concealed hands too early, exchange jokers oddly, discard the tile you need, and that "the computer wins almost every game" ([App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/real-mah-jongg/id1249840399?see-all=reviews); [Google Play, Aug 2026](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.realmahjongg&hl=en_US)). I Love Mahj keeps a KB article titled "the bots cheat" ([KB](https://ilovemahj.com/kb/67000755046)). A Maylee reviewer says every game ended in a wall game ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758261607?see-all=reviews)). Medium.
- **Silent blocks and confusing calls.** New Real Mah Jongg players could not tell why a call was unavailable. NMJL Online needed FAQ threads titled "Why wouldn't the computer let me win?" and "...let me call a discard?" ([FAQ list](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/forumdisplay.php?fid=59)). High.
- **Hidden controls.** Joker swap ([Maylee reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758261607?see-all=reviews)), blank swap ([Mahj Parlour reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761298326?see-all=reviews)), and placing tiles on the rack (an [Eight Bam reviewer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/american-mahjong-practice/id1458051621?see-all=reviews) who asked for tutorial videos). Medium.
- **Small targets and clutter.** Examples:
  - The Mahj App tiles "too tiny" ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756587053)).
  - A tiny Proceed button and a cluttered update with distracting highlights in Mahjong 4 Friends ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-4-friends-multiplayer/id1552704332?see-all=reviews)).
  - Maylee tiles too small on iPad Pro ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758261607?see-all=reviews)).
  - Real Mah Jongg "Not great for phone" ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.realmahjongg&hl=en_US)).
  - Accidental discards from touch controls in Eight Bam ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/american-mahjong-practice/id1458051621?see-all=reviews)).

  Medium.
- **Needing the physical card** ([Mahj Parlour reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761298326?see-all=reviews)) or not getting a digital one ([NMJL Online reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nmjl-online/id6476651173?see-all=reviews)). High that the complaints exist.
- **Suggestions that ignore discards.** An [Eight Bam reviewer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/american-mahjong-practice/id1458051621?see-all=reviews) complains that suggested hands are not limited by discarded tiles, and a [Maylee reviewer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758261607?see-all=reviews) that the "% to win" ignores discards. The [Mahjong 4 Friends docs](https://mahjong4friends.com/pages/faqs/full_documentation.html) say the same of their own list. High.
- **Bugs at the moment of declaring Mah Jongg.** These are the most damaging bugs for a learner:
  - False errors on 2025 wins in Eight Bam, fixed in 9.5 ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/american-mahjong-practice/id1458051621?see-all=reviews)).

Low relevance to a personal app.
- **Official app quality.** NMJL Online: cannot exit, landscape-locked, login problems ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nmjl-online/id6476651173?see-all=reviews)). High that the reviews exist.

### 4.3 What confuses beginners specifically

- **Reading the card.** The official FAQ lists misread color-coding, using a joker as a single or pair, and an exposure on a concealed hand as common reasons a win is blocked ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=105); High). I Love Mahj keeps a page because "There are a few confusing hands on the NMJL 2026 card" ([KB](https://ilovemahj.com/kb/67000755026); High).
- **Card errata and counterfeits.** The 2025 card had color printing errors on at least two lines, and the League announced a reprint ([Sloper column 812, Mar 30, 2025](https://sloperama.com/mahjongg/column2/column812.html); High). Counterfeit cards with wrong hands are sold on Amazon, Temu, and eBay ([Sloper cards FAQ](https://sloperama.com/mjfaq/cards.html); [I Love Mahj KB](https://ilovemahj.com/kb/67000755026); High). The wrong card year selected in the app is another cause of "hands don't match" (same KB).
- **Turn-by-turn uncertainty.** A Maylee reviewer: "I play and still not sure what I'm doing." The developer replied that beginner guidance is something they are "actively thinking about" ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758261607?see-all=reviews); Medium).
- **Waiting on others.** In multiplayer, a win can be blocked because another human has not passed ([FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/showthread.php?tid=105)). This does not apply to solo play.
- **Bad learning material.** Sloper warns that several 2025 to 2026 beginner books, some apparently AI-written, teach wrong rules such as orderly discard rows and flower bonuses. He names *Mah Jongg Made Easy* as the primary rules source ([column 813, Feb 22, 2026](https://sloperama.com/mahjongg/column2/column813.html); High).

## 5. Gaps for a beginner who wants to learn alone

These gaps are inferred from the listings, documentation, and reviews cited in sections 2 to 4. The most load-bearing sources are the [NMJL Online FAQ](https://faqs.nmjlonline.com/forums/), the [Mahjong 4 Friends documentation](https://mahjong4friends.com/pages/faqs/full_documentation.html), [I Love Mahj's learn page](https://ilovemahj.com/learn-american-mah-jongg), the [Eight Bam listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1458051621), and the review pages linked in section 4.

1. **No structured path from scripted scenarios to guided play to unaided play.** Game apps either assume you know the rules (Eight Bam), have short separate tutorials (NMJL Online's four), or teach nothing (Real Mah Jongg, per a forum user). Lesson apps (MAHJ, The Mahj App, Mahj Parlour) add bots, but listings describe lessons and games as separate areas, not guided matches that coach strategy. The nearest pieces are I Love Mahj's Practice Mode leading to Official games, The Mahj Index's Guided and Classic modes, Callie's single guided hand, and NMJL Online's Safety ON/OFF. Confidence Medium: based on listings, not hands-on testing.
2. **Hints say what, not why.** Suggested-hand panels rank by tile overlap and usually ignore dead tiles and exposures. Explanations of why a line is better, which tiles to pass in the Charleston, or which discards are dangerous appear mostly in standalone tools (mahjongg.ai.studio, The Mahj Index drills, Eight Bam Defense) rather than inside a coached game. High for the "ignores discards" part; Medium for the rest.
3. **Blocked moves are often unexplained.** Users value apps that say why (Mahjong 4 Friends, Bam Good Time). The official app's "learn by mistakes" mode lets you err but, per its FAQ, does not coach afterward. High.
4. **Card access versus licensing caution.** The official app and Mahj Parlour hide the card, so a solo learner needs the physical card at hand, and users ask for an in-app reference. Apps that display hands give no license basis (section 6). An integrated view of the current card with highlighting is what solo learners want, and it is exactly where legal risk sits. High.
5. **Bots that teach rather than frustrate.** Bot complaints dominate. Only a few apps reveal what bots held (Eight Bam, Mahj Parlour) or analyze the hand afterward (Jade Table). None was found that explains a bot's decisions in plain language. Medium.
6. **Card-notation literacy is taught outside the game.** Colors meaning suits, X and C markers, values, and line families are explained by web guides (Mahjong Card Guide, The Order of the Tile) and lesson apps. No app was found that drills notation on the live card during a guided game. Medium.
7. **Terminology and "why it's called that."** Lesson apps list terminology and glossaries (The Mahj App, American Mahjong Trainer, MAHJ). None was seen offering short name-origin explanations tied to the tiles in play. Low: based on listing text only.
8. **Phone ergonomics.** Small tiles and buttons, accidental discards, and overlays that cover the hand recur across apps. High.
9. **Trustworthy rules.** Only JokerTile advertises citing a rule source for every answer. Given the bad books Sloper flags, rule explanations that point to *Mah Jongg Made Easy* or the card itself would be distinctive. Medium.

### 6.3 Public reports of NMJL enforcement

**None found.** No cease-and-desist letter, takedown, or lawsuit against an app or website was found in public sources.
- **TMBTQ Law (May 14, 2025):** "There are no widely reported lawsuits" ([TMBTQ](https://www.tmbtq.com/post/ip-and-mah-jongg/); Medium).
- **Lerner David LLP (July 30, 2026):** its copyright article reports none ([Lerner David](https://www.lernerdavid.com/news-successes/unscripted/copyright-issues-in-america-s-fast-growing-mah-jongg-craze); Medium).
- **Sloper and I Love Mahj:** both warn about counterfeit cards on Amazon, Temu, and eBay, but neither describes enforcement ([cards FAQ](https://sloperama.com/mjfaq/cards.html); [KB](https://ilovemahj.com/kb/67000755026)).
- **BBB complaints (2025 to 2026):** they concern undelivered cards and refunds, not enforcement ([BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ny/new-york/profile/games/national-mah-jongg-league-0121-14744/complaints)).

Whether those apps hold private licenses is unknown.

**Search limits.** Reddit was blocked, the web-search budget ran out before targeted searches on specific apps' licensing could run, and no court docket or DMCA-notice database was searched. Absence of reports is therefore Medium confidence, not proof.

### 6.4 Legal commentary (law-firm blogs, not case law)

The two articles differ in emphasis:
- **Lerner David (July 2026)** says game rules and the specific winning hands are uncopyrightable ideas or procedures under 17 U.S.C. § 102(b). In its view, the protectable part is the card's original expression (layout, typography, color scheme, formatting). It advises players not to scan, photocopy, or distribute the card.
- **TMBTQ (May 2025)** reports finding NMJL copyright registrations in the Copyright Office database, including the 2025 card and records back to 1982. It says photocopying a card even for a friend is technically infringing, and that registration makes statutory damages available. It also says unofficial cards with minor changes to layout or fonts may still draw claims.

Neither article addresses apps. Medium confidence for both.

Historical aside: Activision's *Shanghai: Second Dynasty* (1999, developed by Quicksilver Software) was endorsed by the American Mah-Jongg Association, not the NMJL ([Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Shanghai_Second_Dynasty); Low). A 2008 player wrote that she had "the 2008 card programmed in" to it, which suggests users entered new cards themselves ([Sloper bulletin board, April 1, 2008](https://sloperama.com/majexchange/bulletinbd-archive2.htm); Low). If so, it is an early example of the "user enters the card" pattern.

### 6.5 Patterns apps use (observed practice, not a legal conclusion)

1. **Official, no display:** NMJL Online.
2. **Require the user's own card, show only suggestions:** Mahj Parlour, and apparently Real Mah Jongg and I Love Mahj.
3. **User supplies card data that stays on the device:** Jade Table and Callie's (by photo); Shanghai (apparently user-programmed, historically).
4. **Original or user-built cards with an explicit non-affiliation statement:** MyJongg.net, Steam American Mahjong, the I Love Mahj card, American Mahjongg Classic's editor.
5. **Built-in current-year hands with no stated license,** sometimes sold per year: Eight Bam, Mahjong 4 Friends, Bam Good Time, Maylee, Mahjong Mate, The Mahjong App, JLMahJongg.

For our personal, non-commercial app with a privately held 2026 card, pattern 3 is the closest analog: the hands are entered from a purchased card, stored as data, shown in the app's own notation rather than the card's layout, and not distributed. This file reports what others do and what commentators say; it does not settle whether any pattern is permitted.

## 7. Implications for our app

**Borrow.**
- **Strict-but-explained rule enforcement**, with a practice switch modeled on Safety ON/OFF: block illegal moves early, then let the player make mistakes and get feedback. The feedback is the part the official game lacks.
- **A suggested-hands panel** with tiles-away, pin-a-target, concealed marker, and point value. Improve on the field by counting only live tiles (subtract discards and exposures) and adding a one-line reason for each suggestion.
- **Bots that never declare Mah Jongg in early practice** (Eight Bam), then normal bots. Persona names that cue strength (NMJL Online's B-names for Beginner, A-names for Advanced). Reveal bot hands after each game, plus a short post-hand review (Eight Bam, Mahj Parlour, Jade Table).
- **Undo and replay** in guided matches (Eight Bam, Mahjong 4 Friends).
- **Drill formats proven elsewhere:** build a given hand; name a hand from a few tiles; guess a hand from exposures; find the missing tile; choose Charleston passes (I Love Mahj, MyJongg, Eight Bam Defense, The Mahj Index, mahjongg.ai.studio).
- **Win-rate context** so losses to three bots feel fair (I Love Mahj's 22.5% explanation).
- **Accessibility:** big-number tiles, high contrast, a magnifier, large tap targets, and portrait support.
- **Rule explanations that cite their source,** such as *Mah Jongg Made Easy* or "the card says" (JokerTile's approach), with careful handling of confusing 2026 lines.

**Avoid.**
- Hidden joker or blank swaps.
- Constant "Proceed" or pause prompts in solo play.
- Overlays that cover the rack.
- Suggestions that ignore dead tiles.
- Bots that feel scripted or omniscient.
- Landscape lock and other basic usability faults seen in the official app.

## 8. Open questions and weak spots

- **Licenses.** Whether any app holds an NMJL license. None states one, and no NMJL licensee list was found.
- **Card display in Real Mah Jongg and I Love Mahj.** Neither was confirmed; their sites are script-heavy, and help pages did not say.
- **Card notice.** The exact copyright or reproduction notice printed on the 2026 card was not checked.
- **Enforcement search depth.** Reddit was unreadable, the search budget was exhausted before targeted licensing searches ran, and no docket (PACER, CourtListener) or DMCA-notice (Lumen) search was done.
- **MAHJ: Learn American Mahjong and The Mahjong App.** Described only by competitor blogs (Mahj Parlour, Bam Good Time, Mahjong 4 Friends); their store listings were not read.
- **Conflicting numbers.** Maylee showed 148 ratings on one fetch and about 1.2K on another. Eight Bam's Android prices come only from a competitor. NMJL Online's Android availability rests on its FAQ, not a store listing.
- **Not researched:** about nine 2026 apps listed at the end of section 2.2.
- **No hands-on testing.** Interface claims rest on documentation and reviews. A short hands-on pass through NMJL Online's tutorials, Mahj Parlour's lessons, Eight Bam, and Mahjong 4 Friends would firm up the "gaps" section.
